The Sticky Artery Mechanism

The chemicals in cigarettes make the walls of your arteries sticky. This causes fatty material (LDL cholesterol, fibrinogen, cellular debris) to stick to the walls. The fatty material can begin to clog your arteries and reduce the space for blood to flow properly — a process called atherosclerosis.

Heart Attack

If the arteries that carry blood to your heart get clogged, it can lead to a myocardial infarction. Cardiac muscle dies from oxygen deprivation.

Stroke

If the arteries that carry blood to your brain get clogged, it can lead to a stroke. Brain cells begin dying within minutes — permanent disability or death.
